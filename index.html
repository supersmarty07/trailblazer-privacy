Privacy Policy for Trailblazer Effective Date: November 11, 2026 Developer: Trailblazer App Contact: privacy@trailblazerapp.com Trailblazer ("we", "our", or "the App") is an offline-first personal habit, goal, and monthly productivity planner designed with absolute privacy, zero third-party telemetry, and hardware-backed on-device security. 1. Zero Cloud Data Collection Trailblazer operates 100% on-device. We do not collect, upload, sell, or transmit your personal information, tasks, goals, notes, life logs, or device identifiers to any external server or third-party service. No Remote User Accounts: You do not need to create an account or provide an email address to use Trailblazer. No Third-Party Analytics / Trackers: Trailblazer contains no tracking SDKs, no advertisement frameworks, and no telemetry software. No Generative AI Cloud Offloading: All parsing, schedule unpacking, and trophy evaluations run deterministically on your local device CPU. 2. Permissions & How They Are Used Trailblazer requests only the minimum permissions necessary to deliver local user-initiated productivity features: Calendar (android.permission.READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDAR): Purpose: Used strictly when the user chooses to synchronize their monthly recurring tasks or view today's agenda within the app. Data Handling: Calendar data is read and written directly to your device's native calendar provider. Calendar events are never transmitted off your device. Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Purpose: Delivers local, scheduled reminders for your daily brief and urgent task deadlines. Notifications are generated locally by Android AlarmManager. Biometric (android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC): Purpose: Allows unlocking the local application vault using your device's fingerprint or PIN. Authentication is handled by Android BiometricPrompt; biometric data never leaves Android OS secure hardware. 3. On-Device Encryption & Security All application data is stored in a private local SQLite database protected by the Android Keystore. When enabled in the Vault settings, the database file is encrypted using AES-256-GCM with hardware-backed master key wrapping. Screen protection (FLAG_SECURE) can be toggled to prevent other apps and Android recent-apps previews from capturing screenshots of your private planner. 4. Data Portability & Total Erasure (GDPR / APP Compliance) You have complete ownership and control over your data: Export Data: You can export your entire database to unencrypted JSON or an encrypted backup string at any time. Right to Erasure (Delete Everything): You can wipe all tasks, goals, badges, logs, and settings permanently with a single tap in Vault → Security & Privacy → Delete Everything / Reset Template. 5. Children's Privacy Trailblazer does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. 6. Changes to This Policy If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published within the application and at this repository URL. 7. Contact For any questions regarding privacy or data handling: Email: support@trailblazerapp.com
